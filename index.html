FAQ

Questions before you go.

Who can participate?

Most experiences are designed for guests aged 18 and older. Each confirmed departure consists of exactly 35 guests.

Are the trips free?

Yes. Every listed experience is fully sponsored for confirmed guests.

What should I bring?

Pack comfortable clothing, closed-toe shoes, a reusable water bottle, and a small overnight bag.

Where do trips begin?

Participants are picked up from the organization's designated pickup location shown after registration confirmation.

Can I attend alone?

Yes. Solo guests are assigned to a 35-guest group and picked up with the confirmed departure.

What happens after registration?

The confirmation screen displays your reference, packing guidance, and pickup instructions after route verification.

What happens if my plans change?

Submit a new registration while the window is open. Some routes have fixed pickup windows and limited substitutions.
